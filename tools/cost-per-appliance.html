Cost per Appliance Calculator

Compare what different appliances cost to run each month, side by side.

Enter your numbers above and press Calculate. Your result will appear here.

Enter the price from your own bill; prices vary by provider and country.

How this calculator works

The same method as the appliance calculator: power in kilowatts times hours, times days, times the unit price.

Worked example

A 200-watt appliance used 8 hours a day for 30 days uses 48 kWh, costing 16.80 a month at 0.35 per unit, or 201.60 a year.
