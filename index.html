Frequently asked questions

How do I estimate my electricity bill?

Multiply the units you used by the price per unit, then add the fixed charge. The calculator does this for you.

What is a unit of electricity?

A unit is one kilowatt-hour, the energy used by a 1,000-watt appliance running for one hour.

Where do I find my unit price?

On your electricity bill or in your tariff document. Prices vary by provider, country and tariff, so use your own figure.

How much does a fridge cost to run?

It depends on its power and how often the compressor runs. Enter its watts and hours in the appliance calculator.

Does standby power really cost money?

Yes. A device drawing power on standby uses energy all day and night, and several devices together can add up.

How can I lower my bill?

Use less from the largest appliances first, especially heating, cooling and water heating, and check your tariff.

What is the fixed charge?

It is a monthly charge that applies whether you use electricity or not, covering supply and metering. It varies by provider.

Can solar panels lower my bill?

They can reduce the units you buy from the grid, but the saving depends on sunlight, panel size and local rules.
